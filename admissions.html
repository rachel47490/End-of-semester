How to apply

Follow these steps to get started with an application to APESCO.

  1. 01

    Check requirements

    Contact the school office to confirm available places, current entry requirements and deadlines.

  2. 02

    Get the official form

    Request and complete the official application form for the class you are applying to.

  3. 03

    Submit your application

    Return the completed form and documents requested by the school office.

  4. 04

    Follow up

    Follow the school’s instructions for selection, any assessment and confirmation of admission.
