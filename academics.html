Academics

Our academic programmes

At APESCO, learning builds a strong foundation, develops independent thinking and prepares students for the next stage of their education.

Ordinary Level (S.1 to S.4)

Students build a broad foundation across core subjects and develop the knowledge and skills needed for continued study.

Advanced Level (S.5 to S.6)

Students focus on selected subject combinations, deepen their understanding and prepare for further education.

Co-curricular

Sports, clubs and music complement classroom learning, encouraging teamwork, creativity and personal growth.
